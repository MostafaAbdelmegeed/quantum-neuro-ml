# Quantum graduate program shortlist

Last reviewed: 2026-08-01

## Objective and interpretation

This is a first evidence-backed shortlist for Fall 2027, with Fall 2028 as a backup. It is tailored to a candidate with a 4.0 MSc in Data Science, peer-reviewed graph-neural-network research in neuroimaging, strong production ML experience, and no established quantum-computing portfolio yet.

The categories are relative admissions-risk estimates, not guarantees. In particular, no research PhD is a safe option. The 2027 requirements and deadlines must be rechecked as each university publishes its new cycle.

## Profile gap analysis

### Existing advantages

- Excellent recent academic record and research evidence.
- Two peer-reviewed papers and a coherent graph-learning/neuroimaging narrative.
- Senior-level Python, PyTorch, optimization, distributed systems, and production ML experience.
- IBM experience creates a credible connection to the quantum-computing ecosystem.
- Evidence of teaching, leadership, and cross-disciplinary engineering work.

### Priority gaps before applications

1. **Formal quantum foundations:** several attractive programs require transcripted quantum mechanics or evidence equivalent to an upper-level course.
2. **Quantum-computing evidence:** the current CV does not yet show quantum algorithms, circuits, quantum kernels, or variational models.
3. **Theory signal:** strengthen linear algebra, probability, optimization, algorithms, and mathematical writing in the application narrative.
4. **Research bridge:** produce a precise explanation for why graph ML and neuroimaging lead naturally to hybrid quantum graph learning.
5. **Supervisor fit:** research degrees require a short list of faculty whose work genuinely overlaps quantum ML, quantum algorithms, or quantum software.

## Immediate application actions

1. Audit the BSc and MSc transcripts for quantum mechanics, modern physics, electromagnetism, linear algebra, probability, algorithms, and optimization.
2. Collect official course descriptions/syllabi for potentially relevant prior courses; titles alone may not prove prerequisites.
3. Complete a rigorous quantum foundations course and document assessed work, even where a program only counts bachelor-level credits for formal eligibility.
4. Deliver the portfolio project's first reproducible classical baseline and a small quantum-kernel experiment before statements are finalized.
5. Recheck Fall 2027 pages from September through November 2026 and build a deadline tracker as each cycle opens.

## Portfolio alignment

The portfolio project should answer a narrow research question:

> Under a fixed low-sample evaluation protocol, can a hybrid quantum-classical representation of functional brain-connectivity graphs match or improve carefully tuned classical graph-learning baselines?

The minimum publishable-quality package should include a classical feature baseline, a classical GNN baseline, a quantum-kernel model, one variational hybrid model, nested or carefully separated validation, uncertainty estimates, ablations, compute-cost reporting, and a candid limitations section. Publication is possible without a university affiliation, but venue-quality work will require genuine novelty, ethical/licensed data use, strong baselines, and reproducibility.
